UTI SportsWeek 12 Recap

BB's Bold Team moves to 11‑1 and looks unbeatable

Goodyear sends a warning shot, Gunnarrhea pulls off the upset, and BTA Boyz officially become the league's biggest concern.

By GunnarrheaUTI staff writer · Week 12, 2026 · 3 min read

Union of Ten Idiots — BB's Bold Team pushed its win streak to eight and its record to 11-1 on Sunday, and the rest of the league is running out of ideas. Behind them, Goodyear looked like the 2024 champions again, and BTA Boyz lost for the ninth straight week.

The matchup of the week wasn't a matchup

Goodyear 13, Me So Heorny 3

Second against third was billed as a heavyweight fight. Goodyear swept all eight hitting categories, the staff posted a 1.81 ERA, and Me So Heorny hit .217 while throwing pool noodles. The win moves Goodyear into second place.

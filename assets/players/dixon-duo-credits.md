# Dixon Cider player composition

`dixon-ohtani-yordan-cutouts.png` combines adaptations of:

- Yordan Alvarez photograph by Ken Lund:
  https://www.flickr.com/photos/kenlund/48730304951/
- Shohei Ohtani photograph by Joe Glorioso / All-Pro Reels:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dodgers_at_Nationals_(53677190890).jpg

Both original photographs are licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/

The combined artwork is also provided under CC BY-SA 2.0. Changes include
AI-assisted background removal, cropping, resizing, and combining the players
into one transparent composition. The website background is drawn separately
using CSS. No endorsement by the original photographers is implied.

## Three-player banner

`dixon-cider-three-players.png` adds Cam Schlittler to the composition using the
Jeffrey Hyde photograph at https://www.flickr.com/photos/125788546@N06/54476798685/,
also licensed CC BY-SA 2.0. Schlittler's Yankees cap and pinstriped jersey are
AI-assisted uniform edits; the original photograph shows Somerset Patriots apparel.
The three-player composition, including cropping, background removal, scaling,
and arrangement, is likewise provided under CC BY-SA 2.0.

`dixon-cider-three-players-v2.png` revises that composition with consistent
upper-torso framing and softer lower edges; the same credits and license apply.

`dixon-cider-three-players-v3.png` adjusts player spacing to separate Ohtani's
arm from Schlittler's shoulder. The same credits and CC BY-SA 2.0 license apply.

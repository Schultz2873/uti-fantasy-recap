How rewards work

Starter gear is free to wear. Other items unlock from matching official scores, category results, or recorded season finishes. A tied category does not count as a win. League-leading HR ties share the reward. Gear never changes fantasy scoring.

These are proposed achievement rules applied to the 2026 archive, not official league awards. Accounts and server-verified ownership are not connected. Player-inspired gear is a tribute design, not an affiliation or replica of memorabilia.

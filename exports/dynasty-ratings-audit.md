## Remaining limits

- No independent future projections or prospect scouting model.
- High source ranks can keep young pitchers high despite limited MLB history.
- No expert baseline leaves a player unrated, even with strong production.
- Missing seasons reduce production weight rather than counting as zero performance.
- The slot adjustment assumes available players can fill extra roster slots; actual owner cuts, positions, IL, and minors capacity can differ.
- Pick values remain manual estimates.

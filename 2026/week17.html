Union of Ten Idiots: Week 17 Recap
Fantasy Baseball Weekly Recap

The Throne Changes Hands and the 13 Game Losing Streak Finally Snaps

Goodyear claimed the No. 1 seed, Dixon Cider looked ready to defend her crown, and John’s Super Team became the bum who finally lost to BTA Boyz.

By: Gunnarrhea Week: 17 Feature: Matchup-by-matchup recap

Week 17 delivered a new No. 1 seed, the end of a historic losing streak, and enough embarrassment to carry this league into the playoffs. Goodyear Gila Monsters completely demolished You Don't Know Bo 15–1 and stole the throne after BB's Bold Team choked away a close matchup against Gunnarrhea. Dixon Cider continued looking like a serious threat to reclaim the championship by beating Me So Heorny 12.5–3.5, while Mactown MacDaddies and This Is Mizerable combined their opposite weaknesses into a perfectly disappointing 8–8 tie. But the biggest story belonged to BTA Boyz, who finally ended their 13-game losing streak by defeating John's Super Team and permanently attaching Johnboy's name to one of the most humiliating moments in league history. The playoffs are almost here, the contenders are getting hot, and the frauds are beginning to expose themselves.

Dixon Cider vs Me So Heorny

Result: Dixon Cider turned Me So Heorny into Me So Humiliated.
Dixon Cider 12.5 – 3.5 Me So Heorny

Daaaaang Dixon Cider was dealing straight cheese in Week 17, dominating on both sides of the ball and reminding the league why she is the reigning champion. Dixon crushed the offensive categories with 55 runs, 17 home runs, 57 RBI, a .290 average, and 169 total bases. The pitching staff joined the beating with a 3.43 ERA, 1.231 WHIP, six wins, six quality starts, and a .237 BAA. I had a chance to schedule a quick interview with Dixon Cider following Week 17's win and she made the following statement: "Me So Heorny might have a chance if they brought a girl in to help run their team..... Oh wait, I forgot they already have two girls in charge. They're cooked". Bold words, but is she wrong? Rumor around the league that these are the facts. But either way, after taking 12.5 categories, she can way whatever she wants. Dixon is officially back to reeling, dealing, and preparing to defend her crown in the playoffs.

Me So Heorny really did not have the best week. Hitting was pretty solid but nothing to write home about. They we're just kinda outta luck with the overwhelming offense of Dixon Cider. What killed em was the stinky pitching. They took K's with 88 and Saves with 5, rest of them not even being competitive. They posted a a 5.08 ERA with five losses, while producing only two quality starts across 79.2 innings Yeah it's probably just an off week, but this kind of inconsistency has got to make them a little nervous considering it's almost show time! But not to worry, it's amost time for y'all to lose in fantasy football too!!

League takeaway: Dixon Cider looks ready to defend the title. Me So Heorny looks ready to defend themselves in the group chat.

Mactown MacDaddies vs This is Mizerable

Result: Ties are boring, both y'all stink, do better
Mactown MacDaddies 8 – 8 This is Mizerable

The MacDaddies once again received enough offense to win and once again watched the pitching staff drag them back into mediocrity. Mactown took six hitting categories behind 45 runs, 11 home runs, 40 RBI, 40 walks, and four net stolen bases, but the rotation responded with a 5.22 ERA, only 33 strikeouts, and two quality starts across just 39.2 innings. I have said it for about 35 straight weeks: until the MacDaddies fix this pitching staff, they will remain permanently trapped in the middle of the standings. And on top of all that, they traded away their biggest name pitcher as the MacDaddies are trying to collect the complete set of Phillies. Which is fair to be honest. Guy just became a Cub so it's kinda like "Ehhhh, kinda gross". But he is trading him to Goodyear so the guy is probably going to win Cy Young this year.

This is Mizerable followed the exact opposite formula, relying on its pitching staff to rescue a lifeless offensive performance. The rotation delivered a 2.78 ERA, 1.065 WHIP, 70 strikeouts, eight quality starts, and a dominant .176 BAA, yet somehow recorded only one win in 71.1 innings. One additional win would have flipped the matchup, but naturally Mizerable found a way to leave it on the table. That's the This is Mizerable we know and love! And as of now hey technically escaped with a tie, but I got this crazy feeling that later this week, 2 of This is Mizerable's hits are going to be taken away and the TB category is going to end in a tie and flip to a MacDaddy win. Now I ain't no future teller, I'm just a guy with a feeling. But somehow, This is Mierable will still blow this. Watch

League takeaway: Mactown has no pitching, Mizerable has no offense, and together they created one perfectly balanced pile of disappointment.

Result: Goodyear ripped the No. 1 seed away from BB and left Bo in the dust.
Goodyear Gila Monsters 15 – 1 You Don't Know Bo

AND THE NEW 2026 1ST SEED IS ..... GOODYEAR!!! Back to reclaim the throne after absolutely annilating the 2025 GM of the Year, You Don't Know Bo. Shiiii this is what I woulda done to Bo last week too, but like I said, I didn't want him to leave the league. But you on the other hand didn't care as you piled up 56 runs, 15 home runs, 159 total bases, and a .259 average, while the pitching staff added a 2.91 ERA, 1.015 WHIP, seven quality starts, and six net saves. The pitching was not nearly good enough to rescue them either, managing just two quality starts with a 3.92 ERA and 1.343 WHIP. That stings. Not very GM of the Year of you Patty. But honestly there was nothing to be done. Not because of Goodyear's solid performance, but because you trash. Not John's Super Team trash, more like the Gunnar's self given haircut trash.

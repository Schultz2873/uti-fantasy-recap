Union of Ten Idiots: Week 18 Recap
Fantasy Baseball Weekly Recap

Goodyear Is on Fire and Everybody Else Is a Fucking Mess

The Gila Monsters keep steamrolling the league, two matchups end in completely useless ties, John's offense gets betrayed by its own pitching staff, and Bo finds another way to stay out of the playoff picture.

By: Gunnarrhea Week: 18 Feature: Matchup-by-matchup recap

Week 18 is officially in the books, and somehow the closer we get to the playoffs, the more stupid this league gets. Goodyear is out here beating the absolute shit out of everybody, following up last week's 15–1 massacre with another convincing win over Gunnarrhea. Meanwhile, BB and Dixon spent seven days battling just to finish 8–8, and Mactown and BTA decided that looked like so much fun that they did the exact same thing. Great work, everybody.

Then we have John's Super Penis putting together one of the best offensive weeks in the league only for his pitching staff to respond with one win, one quality start and zero Net Saves. Me So Heorny happily took advantage, while This is Mizerable survived one of its worst pitching weeks in months because the whipped cream titty man couldn't get the job done. With the playoffs getting closer, some teams are heating up, some are falling apart, and some apparently still think PCA is the missing piece to a dynasty. Let's get into it.

New Weekly Category: ERA

Goodyear Gila Monsters vs Gunnarrhea

Result: Goodyear cured Gunnarrhea
Goodyear Gila Monsters 11 – 5 Gunnarrhea

Goodyear Gila Monsters found some of those anti-Gunnarrhea pills that most of the league has had access to this season, because they put the throttle on high early and never let off the gas. Man oh man, is this team on fire right now as they wouldn't even let Gunnarrhea come close to sniffing a lead all week! Seriously, there wasn't even a moment where I thought i could come back. From start to end, it too much domination. Almost as dominant as The BTA Boyz would be with PCA right now. And it's not just one side carrying them either — both their hitting and pitching were unmatched. Goodyear scored 60 runs, blasted 17 home runs, and then completely buried Gunnarrhea on the mound. Across 80.1 innings, they posted a ridiculous 2.13 ERA, 1.021 WHIP and .191 BAA, along with 74 strikeouts, 7 wins and 9 quality starts. That's INSANE. Nobody can compete with that right now.

Luckily, Gunnarrhea's offense actually got hot enough late in the week to keep this from turning into a complete embarrassment. They hit .273, collected 87 hits, drew 30 walks and even beat out Goodyear in total bases 150–146. Suck it twerp. But the pitching staff just couldn't come close to matching what Goodyear was doing, finishing with a 4.10 ERA, 1.176 WHIP and .241 BAA, along with only 3 wins and 3 quality starts. Gunnarrhea will take the domination in NS 8–1 though. Somehow we managed to steal five categories, but let's be real — this was still a straight-up blowout with very little hope of Gunnarrhea ever taking control.

League takeaway: Goodyear has followed up last week's 15–1 destruction of Bo with another 11–5 win, putting them at 26–6 over the last two weeks. The Gila Monsters aren't just hot anymore — they're becoming a serious problem.

BB's Bold Team vs Dixon Cider

Result: BB and Dixon fought for seven days just to accomplish absolutely nothing
BB's Bold Team 8 – 8 Dixon Cider

A tie is definitely not what BB was looking for while trying to get back into that 1 seed, but I'm sure he's not too upset considering it came against the reigning 2025 champ. BB's offense did most of the heavy lifting this week, putting up 50 runs, 13 home runs and 42 RBI while taking AVG .244 to .232 and total bases 117–107. The pitching wasn't terrible either, but I do have one question: how does a roster with 4 relief pitchers finish the entire week with 1 Net Save? That stings my man. Winning that category would have made the entire difference.

Dixon Cider had a VERY sluggish offensive week by her standards. She finished with only 8 home runs, and 4 of them came on Sunday, which tells you just how dead that lineup was for most of the matchup. Are the veterans finally starting to hit a wall? We're definitely not used to seeing this little offensive production from Dixon. Luckily, the pitching staff bailed her out with a 3.46 ERA, 1.262 WHIP and .231 BAA, while also taking quality starts 6–4 and Net Saves 3–1. Still, this might be the clearest sign yet that it's finally time to sell Shohei. That's even if anyone wants him at this point.

League takeaway:

BB needed a win for the race to the 1 seed and Dixon needed to remind everyone why she's the defending champ. Instead, they split everything down the middle and wasted an entire week of everyone's time. 8–8. Nobody wins. Nobody's happy. Perfect. Thanks you bums

Mactown MacDaddies vs BTA Boyz

Result: Mactown couldn't pitch, BTA couldn't hit, and both walked away with that tie
Mactown MacDaddies 8 – 8 BTA Boyz

Mactown's offense actually showed some life this week, putting up 41 runs, 12 home runs, 38 RBI and 48 walks while hitting .242. They pretty much handled BTA offensively across the board and finished with a 116–94 advantage in total bases. Unfortunately, apparently nobody told the pitching staff there was a matchup going on. Mactown threw just 41.1 innings with a 4.79 ERA, 1.524 WHIP and .299 BAA. Letting opponents hit damn near .300 against you is certainly one way to make sure a winnable week ends in a tie. Gosh is that staff HURTING.

Hey now BTA Boyz, this is two weeks in a row where we didn't chalk up tally's in the L column. I wish i could have said it was a two game winning streak, but we'll take what we can get, right? Especially with the offense being disgusting. They hit a trash ERA of .206 with just 37 runs, 10 home runs and 28 RBI. Good thing they have so many pitchers, because that's the only reason this wasn't another loss. BTA threw 69.2 innings with a solid 3.49 ERA, 1.163 WHIP and .208 BAA, while taking wins 6–3 and quality starts 5–2. So once again, the arms dragged the corpse of this offense across the finish line. Maybe PCA can hit for all 13 spots in the lineup if they ever get him.

League takeaway: This was the perfect 8–8 tie: Mactown had an offense but forgot how to pitch, while BTA had pitching but forgot baseball also requires hitting. Put these two teams together and we might finally have one complete roster.

Me So Heorny vs John's Super Team

Result: Heorny stole one hitting category, swept the mound, and let John's offense waste a masterpiece.
Me So Heorny 9 – 7 John's Super Team

Me So Heorny gobbled up John's Super Penis this week with a 9-7 win. It wasn't very pretty, but those kinda wins are what matters. The offense was uhhhhhh FOOKING TRASH. Maybe if they didn't trade away PCA, the win would have been more lopsided, because they got their ass handed to them in nearly every hitting category. They managed just 33 runs, 9 home runs and 33 RBI while hitting .246, but somehow stealing SBN 6–5 was all they needed. The pitching staff did the rest — and by the rest, I mean literally everything. Heorny swept every single pitching category behind a 3.26 ERA, 1.188 WHIP and .227 BAA, while piling up 79 strikeouts, 5 quality starts and 5 Net Saves. The offense basically handed the pitchers a 1–7 deficit and said, "You guys figure it out." Somehow, they did.

John's Super Penis might have to switch his team name to the Ice Man with how cold and unlucky this team has been. Actually, that team name would be pretty COLD. Despite the loss, John's offense had an insane week, finishing with 45 runs, 16 home runs, 46 RBI, 39 walks, a .260 AVG and 143 total bases. They basically dominated every offensive category outside of SBN. Unfortunately, the pitching staff completely shit the bed and wasted all of it. John's arms finished with a 4.84 ERA, 1.448 WHIP, get fucking this..... 1 win, 1 quality start and 0 Net Saves. Johnboy are you kidding me?!?! ONE WIN, ONE QUALITY START AND ZERO NET SAVES + HOLDS?!?!?! Brother..... Imagine winning seven of eight hitting categories and still losing the week. That's not it my man

League takeaway: John's offense won 7 of 8 hitting categories and still found a way to lose. Heorny needed exactly one category from the bats and then let the pitching staff drag the entire team to a 9–7 win. Somewhere, John's hitters are filing a grievance against their own pitching staff.

This is Mizerable vs You Don't Know Bo

Result: Mizerable's pitching finally sucked for once and Bo still couldn't beat them
This is Mizerable 9 – 7 You Don't Know Bo

This is Mizerable is getting nasty with it again as they take down the whipped cream titty man himself, You Don't Know Bo. And this comes after Mizerable had an incredibly off week on the mound, which rarely happens considering they've had some of the most dominant pitching numbers in the league over the past couple of months. This week was the complete opposite, finishing with a 5.43 ERA, 1.438 WHIP and .269 BAA. Luckily, the offense showed up when the pitching finally didn't. Mizerable dropped 50 runs, 13 home runs and 50 RBI, while adding 31 walks, 5 SBN and 132 total bases. They took six of the eight hitting categories, which was just enough to overcome the pitching staff fumbling the bag and steal a 9–7 win.

Bo stays outta the playoff race with another L. The crazy part is his pitching was actually really good this week, posting a 3.05 ERA, 1.065 WHIP and .197 BAA, and it took five of the eight pitching categories. The offense, though, couldn't take advantage of Mizerable's rare pitching collapse. Bo managed just 34 runs, 7 home runs and 33 RBI, losing six hitting categories despite batting a solid .279. It really does look like the only team he can beat is unfortunately, Gunnarrhea. Until the playoffs come around baby! Then you're mine. Oh wait just kidding, you're not gonna make it bitch.

League takeaway: Mizerable finally gave somebody an opening on the mound and Bo responded by scoring 34 runs and hitting 7 bombs. If you can't beat Mizerable when their pitching puts up a 5.43 ERA, when exactly are you planning on doing it?
